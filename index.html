About
IPS

A studio that ships its own products, then works on yours.

Directed by Matthew Hartley, Imagine Peak Studios builds and runs its own experience platform — Realty Wave for real estate, Harmonic Wave for music — and brings the same product discipline to client work.

Experience Platform

One AI-native platform. Many stories.

The studio's experience platform turns a subject — a song, a place, a property — into connected media and interactive experiences. Each brand applies the platform to one market, with human review before publication.

Conversational agents

In Realty Wave, local guides people can call or message anytime, grounded in sourced information, with an emailed summary of what they discussed.

AI-assisted production

Films, Reels, portrait shorts, and lyric videos made through an automated workflow, with human review at key stages.

Hosts

AI presenters with a consistent look and voice, who front the films and carry the story from one format to the next.

Original music

For place-based experiences, songs written and produced for the area, like a sing-along about a day in a beach town.

Experience pages

One page for each experience, bringing the film, the music, the context, and the sources together.

Distribution

Preparing releases for video, social, and compatible AI channels, with publishing support scoped to each project.

Music · Taking Projects

Harmonic Wave

A platform for telling the story behind a song. Harmonic Wave builds a Host-led show around a track — film, lyric video, and portrait shorts — and the artist approves how their music is presented before production begins.

An automated production workflow, with human review at key stages, brings the song, the Host, and the visual story together. The creator's context stays with the music across films, shorts, and discovery through compatible AI agents. It was the platform's first brand and the proof of the studio's method.
