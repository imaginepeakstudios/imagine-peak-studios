Flagship Product · Waitlist Open

Harmonic Wave

An AI-native music platform. Harmonic Wave turns creator-approved release materials — artist bio, release notes, DSP links, visual identity — into agent-readable experiences, so artists and rights holders control how their music is presented by AI instead of leaving it to whatever answers the question.

Context, storytelling, and creative intent are defined once and carried by any agent that speaks the Model Context Protocol. It is the studio's flagship product and the proof of its method.

Not yet generally available. The waitlist is open and we are running early demos.
